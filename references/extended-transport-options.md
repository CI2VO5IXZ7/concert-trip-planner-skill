# Extended Transport Options

## Purpose

This is a reference for 特种兵行程的扩展交通方式. Focus: fast, cheap, durable. 只保留能打仗的方式，不做舒适旅行建议。

## Allowed Transport Modes

### Train / High-Speed Rail

- 包含: 高铁 / 动车 / 普快 / 夜车硬座 or 硬卧 when needed.
- Usage: 适合 city-to-city 主干线, especially when airport access is weak or station is closer to the venue.
- Search method: 优先查官方 12306 / FlyAI train search / 平台余票. Filter by departure window, arrival window, and seat class.
- Pros: 稳定、准点率高、city center access 好、可中途补觉.
- Cons: 旺季抢票难, late-night seats may be limited, long routes can be tiring.
- Notes: Keep the station buffer in mind; if it is a long transfer, prefer earlier arrival.

### Flight

- Usage: 长距离跨城, time first, cost second.
- Search method: FlyAI flight search / official airline app / OTA compare. Check departure time, arrival time, baggage rules, and airport transfer time.
- Pros: 最快覆盖长距离, good for same-day cross-region trips.
- Cons: 安检 + 候机 + 延误 risk, airport access can be costly at night.
- Notes: For tight plans, only choose when the airport buffer is realistic.

### Ride Share (顺风车)

- Includes: 滴滴顺风车, 哈啰顺风车, and similar合法拼车/顺路搭乘 options.
- Usage: 适合 local-to-local or station/airport feeder legs when public transit is weak.
- Search method: 直接在平台搜出发地/目的地 + 时间窗口. Use multiple time slots because order availability is not fixed.
- Pros: 便宜, door-to-door, useful for late-night or weak-transit segments.
- Cons: 时间不确定性高, driver may cancel, route may change, safety depends on person and platform state.
- Notes: 
  - 先看司机接单率、行程数、评分.
  - 尽量白天/人多区域上车.
  - Share trip details with a friend, keep in-app communication, and avoid off-platform payment.
  - If timing is critical, treat it as a fallback not a guarantee.

### Self-Drive (自驾/租车)

- Includes: 租车平台短租、自驾换乘、少量城市内跨点 driving.
- Usage: When public transit is too weak and the route is simple enough to drive.
- Search method: Search rental platforms, compare pickup/return points, check driving license requirements, mileage limits, fuel/charging policy, and insurance.
- Pros: 灵活, can cover dead zones, useful for multi-stop routes.
- Cons: 疲劳驾驶 risk, parking cost, traffic uncertainty, return logistics.
- Notes:
  - 强制留出休息时间, no all-night solo driving when avoidable.
  - 轮换驾驶 if multiple people can drive.
  - Do not choose self-drive when arrival time is critical and rest is insufficient.

### Overnight Bus / Night Bus

- Usage: 夜间跨城, usually when price matters and arrival can be early morning.
- Search method: 查长途汽车站 / 官方票务 / 城际班车信息. Look for midnight or early-morning departures.
- Pros: 价格低, can save one night of lodging, often direct.
- Cons: 舒适度低, sleep quality poor, delays and road conditions affect arrival.
- Notes: Pack light, keep valuables on body, and treat arrival time as approximate.

### Airport Overnight

- Usage: 适合 red-eye departures, long layovers, or waiting for first wave flights.
- Search method: 查机场官网 / 机场公众号 / 实测用户攻略 for terminal opening hours and 24h areas.
- Pros: 免费 or low-cost waiting, closer to early flights than city lodging.
- Cons: Lighting/noise, limited seating, security checks, and some zones close at night.
- Notes:
  - 先确认 terminal 是否 24h 开放, then find rest zones, benches, or designated waiting areas.
  - Keep power bank, documents, and jacket ready.
  - Do not rely on sleeping in restricted areas.

### Train Station Overnight

- Usage: Late arrivals before first train / waiting for overnight or early trains.
- Search method: 查车站候车室开放时间、是否有 24h waiting area, and platform access rules.
- Pros: Close to train departure, cheaper than moving around at night.
- Cons: 人流混杂, noise, limited resting posture.
- Notes:
  - Use station seating/rest zones only.
  - Keep ticket/ID accessible for re-entry or boarding checks.
  - If the station closes sections at night, move to an allowed public waiting area early.

### Multi-Leg Transit (多段联运)

- Usage: Combine cheap and fast legs to hit the schedule, e.g. 飞机 + 地铁, 高铁 + 顺风车, 夜巴 + local transit.
- Search method: Search each leg separately, then stitch by buffer. First lock the most time-sensitive leg, then fill feeder legs.
- Pros: 更灵活, can reduce total cost while protecting arrival time.
- Cons: 每一段都会加风险; missed connection can break the whole trip.
- Notes:
  - Prefer station/airport-first design: fixed long leg first, local leg second.
  - Never stack two fragile transfers with zero slack.
  - If any leg is uncertain, downgrade the whole plan.

## Excluded Modes

- 包车
- 专车

Why excluded: 费用过高，不符合特种兵精神. These options are too expensive and too comfort-oriented for this guide.

## Transfer Buffer Recommendations

- 特种兵标准: 最少 30-60 分钟.
- Venue: 60 minutes.
- Airport: 90 minutes.
- Train station: 45 minutes.
- Local transfer: 20 minutes.

If the plan needs a tighter buffer, treat it as high risk.

## Risk Notes

- 顺风车: availability may vanish, driver may cancel, and ETA can move.
- 夜间出行: reduce visibility and safety margin; keep route simple.
- 自驾: fatigue is a hard stop; do not force long solo drives.
- 夜巴: arrival time can drift; do not stack with a hard deadline.
- 过夜 waiting: use airport/train station public areas only; no hotel planning here.
- Multi-leg plans: one missed link can invalidate the full chain.
